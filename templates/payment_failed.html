Common Issues & Solutions:
🔴 "Limit Exceeded" Error

Your UPI app has a daily transaction limit. Try again tomorrow or use a different payment method.

🔴 Money Debited But Transaction Failed

The amount will be automatically refunded to your account within 24-48 hours.

🔴 UPI App Not Opening

Make sure you have a UPI app installed (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm). Try opening the payment link again.

🔴 Wrong UPI PIN

Double-check your PIN and try again. You can reset it in your UPI app settings.

💡 Tip: If money was debited from your account, please contact us with your transaction ID. We'll verify and unlock your feature manually.
